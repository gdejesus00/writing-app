    Spelling and grammar

    Harper checks your writing on this device, so your words never leave it. The first time, it downloads about 20 MB, then it works offline. Tap an underlined word to see the fix. For a deeper check, use the Claude button.

    Google Drive

    Your stories go in a “Writing App” folder in your Drive, with one folder per story and one .md file per page. The app gets your latest pages when you open it or come back to it, and saves to Drive as you write and when you leave.

    Read aloud

    Reading starts at the sentence your cursor is in, or at the top if your cursor is at the end. Select some text to hear just that part.

    Dictation

    Tap the mic and talk. Pause, then say “new paragraph”, “new line”, “period”, “comma” or “question mark” on its own to add it. Your browser turns speech into text online: Edge on your PC uses Microsoft, and Chrome on your phone uses Google. On your phone, the microphone key on your keyboard also types by voice.
